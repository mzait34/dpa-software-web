3 · Segmentation

One click: finds every tooth, draws its outline, and opens Editing so you can check the result. Everything below is for correcting it by hand.

Automatic beta

Places one marker per tooth from the crown tips. Check them: add a missing tooth with a click, remove an extra one by clicking it.

Manual

Click once on the occlusal surface of each tooth. Click a marker again to remove it.

4 · Editing

Select: click a tooth on the model to pick it, drag to turn the model. Brush / Eraser: drag to paint. Sculpt / Smooth: drag the circle along the selected tooth's margin to move it or even it out. With any tool, right-drag turns the model and Alt+click picks a tooth.

5 · Tooth numbers

Check the numbers on the crowns. If a tooth is missing, click its place in the chart (✕) and the teeth behind it renumber.

Contacts & IPR

Auto alignment

Lines the crowns up along an ideal arch curve (white), side by side from the midline, each turned to face out of the curve. Heights, tip and torque stay as planned. It sets the goal being edited.

Bolton analysis

Review

Literature-based rules of thumb (predicted vs. achieved aligner studies), not a validated prediction. Click a reference for the source.

Bite shift

Moves a whole arch as one block (e.g. mandibular advancement, midline or vertical correction), spread over the aligners. The occlusal map and contacts include it.

Opening turns about an estimated hinge axis (condyles about 60 mm behind and 35 mm above the arch centre), not a measured one.

Extraction & pontics

Click a tooth to extract it (click again to restore). P puts a pontic in an empty place between two teeth: a tooth-shaped filler for the aligner, mirrored from the other side, that narrows as the space closes.

Arch analysis

Measured from the crowns at the first aligner: how the incisors meet, how wide the arch is, the room it is short of, and Pont's guide.

Elastics & buttons

Click to add, then click the model: once for a button or a miniscrew, twice for an elastic. Each part goes on at the aligner shown and stays until the one you set. Right-clicking an aligner on the timeline adds one from there.

Clinical photographs

Settings

Printed on the patient's treatment video and on the IPR report, beside the logo.

Every case you check and accept teaches the segmentation where a crown ends on your scanner. Only the shape measurements of a few thousand triangles are kept, under a one-way id: no scan, no name, no serial number, and nothing leaves this browser.

The model is used on a new scan only while it beats the plain geometry on cases it was not trained on, so a half-trained one cannot make the segmentation worse.

Training data for AI segmentation

Each scan with its checked tooth outlines, in the Teeth3DS format (OBJ + per-vertex FDI labels), named by the case serial, never the patient's name. Collected cases are what an AI model for this clinic's scans is trained on.

3D viewer for the patient

One HTML file with the whole plan in 3D: the patient opens it on a phone or computer (no app, works offline), plays it aligner by aligner and turns the model. Send it by WhatsApp or e-mail, or upload it to a web host to get a link.
